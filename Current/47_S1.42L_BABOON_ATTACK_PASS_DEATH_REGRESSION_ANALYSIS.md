## User-observed result

The previously open reverse-direction gate **Pikmin -> Baboon Hawk** passed:
- thrown Pikmin can latch onto a living Baboon Hawk;
- they attack normally;
- they can kill the Hawk.

A new post-death regression was observed:
- when the Hawk dies from Pikmin attacks, the Pikmin that were latched to it disappear;
- the intended dead Hawk body does not remain visibly available as expected.

The user clarified the desired corpse behavior:
- the SellBodies dead Baboon Hawk body must remain;
- it must remain a carryable object;
- Pikmin must be able to be thrown onto the corpse and carry it toward the Onion;
- players may also carry it;
- living Baboon Hawks should **not** pick up and relocate that corpse.

## Log-confirmed findings

**CONFIRMED**
- `Kill enemy called! destroy: False` occurs for the killed Hawk.
- After that death marker, Pikmin continue producing attack activity against the old `BaboonHawkEnemy(Clone)` target instead of cleanly finishing the attack/latch task.
- `Leader is null when following` count remains **0**.
- SellBodies creates a `BaboonHawkBody(Clone)`.
- living Baboon Hawks later log `Grabbing item!!! BaboonHawkBody(Clone)` three times.
- the session later records `ShipOnion: saved 35 pikmin to disk on despawn`, so the missing attacking Pikmin were not shown by this log to have been permanently deleted from persistence.

## Root-cause assessment

**STRONGLY SUPPORTED / IMPLEMENTATION BASIS**

The failure is a death-cleanup problem, not a failure of the newly restored attack direction:

1. Pikmin remain associated with the original dead Hawk attack/latch target after death.
2. SellBodiesFixed replaces the practical corpse interaction with its carryable `BaboonHawkBody` item after its configured delay and removes/moves the old enemy representation from normal play.
3. Pikmin still attached to the stale original target therefore disappear from the visible play area with that target.
4. Separately, because the replacement corpse is a `GrabbableObject`, normal Baboon-Hawk scrap AI can treat it as collectible scrap and move it.

This is why the fix must **not** disable SellBodies corpse generation: the replacement corpse is the object Pikmin are intended to carry to the Onion.

## Closed interaction rule after this evidence

The live interaction direction is now permanently recorded as:

- **Baboon Hawk -> Pikmin:** blocked/ignored — PASS.
- **Pikmin -> living Baboon Hawk:** normal attack/latch allowed — PASS.

Do not restore the old S1.42J two-way zero-interaction rule.
