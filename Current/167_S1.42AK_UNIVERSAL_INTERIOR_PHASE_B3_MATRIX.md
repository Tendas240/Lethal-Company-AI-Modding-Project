The authoritative matrix contains exactly **30 pairing-target moons × 53 selectable interiors = 1,590 cells**.

Classification is conservative and evidence-derived:

1. **Offense** uses the exact S1.42AK runtime row reconciled in Phase B2.
2. The **22 DIRECT_LLL_UNIVERSAL_TAG_OVERRIDE** flows are `VIABLE_EQUAL_100` on the 12 Vanilla and 16 Custom target moons because the active rule is `Vanilla:100,Custom:100`, followed by the accepted S1.42AB effective-weight normalizer.

4. The two **External** target moons — Black Mesa and Oxyde — remain `NOT_YET_PROVEN` for all flows unless pairing-specific evidence exists. Neither the LLL `Vanilla/Custom` tag override nor Black Mesa's `lethal_company:vanilla/custom` rule is extrapolated onto External moons.
5. The 27 owner/asset-controlled Custom flows remain `NOT_YET_PROVEN` outside exact observed/explicitly restricted cells.
6. **Shatteredrooms × Experimentation** and **Shatteredrooms × Embrion** are `AUTHOR_OR_OWNER_HARD_BLOCK` from the explicit current owner restriction.
7. No cell is called `KNOWN_TECHNICAL_RESTRICTION` without technical compatibility evidence, and no cell is called `CONFIG_GAP` merely because evidence is incomplete.

A `VIABLE_EQUAL_100` matrix cell is an **availability/effective-weight statement**, not proof that the exact pairing has already completed generation/traversal/geometry/elevator/routing/NavMesh acceptance.

## What Phase B3 proves

Phase B3 separates three different questions that must not be conflated:

- **Current broad availability already established:** 662 cells.
- **Current owner-side rejection already established:** 14 cells.
- **Unknown current pairing status:** 914 cells.

This means the project does **not** need a blanket weight rewrite. The accepted equal-weight normalizer is already correct after viability filtering, and 22 direct LLL flows plus Black Mesa already have broad Vanilla/Custom availability rules. The unresolved work is concentrated in owner-controlled and External-moon pairings.

## Phase C entry condition

Phase C must investigate proposed restriction removals and unproven owner-controlled pairings before any gameplay candidate is authored. Compatibility proof must cover, as applicable:

- main entrance / fire-exit or equivalent entrance pairing;
- no mismatched or stranded teleport targets;
- successful dungeon generation without exhausted retries/fatal errors;
- entrance and normal traversal;
- door/socket geometry;
- clipping/inaccessible required geometry;
- elevators/ladders/special traversal;
- routing and NavMesh;
- no duplicate registration.

High-risk or explicit owner-hard-block cells require targeted runtime proof before removal.
