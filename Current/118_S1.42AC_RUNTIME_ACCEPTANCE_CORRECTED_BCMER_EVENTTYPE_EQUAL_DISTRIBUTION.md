## Why the historical rejection is no longer the current decision

`Current/106...` rejected S1.42AC because it interpreted the eight logged lines

`Set eventType weight for <type> to <value>`

as eight aggregate EventType weights and required them to be numerically equal.

`Current/109...` subsequently proved from exact BCMER `1.71.0` source that those logged values are **per-event weights**, intentionally divided by the enabled-event population of each EventType. BCMER later selects individual `MEvent` instances from the weighted pool. Equal aggregate EventType probability therefore requires unequal per-event weights whenever EventType populations differ.

The historical rejection remains preserved as evidence of the original runtime observation and decision. This acceptance is a later explicit decision under the corrected model; it does not rewrite history.

## Scope boundary preserved

This acceptance does **not** claim mathematically exact long-run `12.5%` final executed frequency after all event-specific eligibility filters. BCMER's `ChooseEvents()` can modify the eligible pool through moon restrictions, event incompatibilities, forced events, without-replacement behavior and related runtime rules.

If exact post-filter executed frequency is ever required, that is a separate algorithm-design scope and must not be approximated by forcing equal per-event weights or inverse-compensating the current config.
