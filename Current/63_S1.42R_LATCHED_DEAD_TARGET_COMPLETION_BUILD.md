Exact patched method:

`LethalMin.Pikmin.AttackEnemyTask.IntervaledUpdate()`

1. the exact task is currently latched;

3. that exact enemy's `enemyScript.isEnemyDead` is true.

`PikminAI.FinishTaskServerRpc()`
