## Dusk 0.9.25 evaluation semantics

For `MapObjectSpawnMechanics` with `PrioritiseMoons=true`, the effective selection order is:

1. exact Moon key;
2. exact Interior key fallback;
3. matching Moon-tag curves;
4. zero when no applicable source exists.

Moon and Interior curves are not combined. Interior-tag entries are not separately tag-evaluated in this mode.

S1.42AE must validate both exact 18-key tables and all contained curves, log both tables, then mutate only the 18 Moon/tag curves by `SpawnScale=0.5` (values plus in/out tangents). The 18 Interior curves are validation-only and must remain byte-for-byte logically unmodified at runtime.

This preserves Dusk as owner of spawn evaluation, preserves relative Moon/tag distribution, and halves the effective amplitude for both exact-Moon and averaged Moon-tag paths without broad global hooks.
