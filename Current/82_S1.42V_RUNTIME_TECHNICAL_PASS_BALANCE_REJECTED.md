## LateGameUpgrades scaling decision

More Ship Upgrades / LateGameUpgrades `3.14.1` uses percentage multipliers:

- Jet Fuel currently `20%` initial / `20%` incremental on `jetpackAcceleration`;
- Jetpack Thrusters currently `20%` initial / `20%` incremental on the separate maximum-power/speed value.

Jet Fuel already scales automatically with the new 16f base: one 20% tier adds `3.2f` instead of the `2.4f` it added to the S1.42V 12f base. Therefore Jet Fuel remains `20 / 20` to avoid unnecessary over-buffing.

Jetpack Thrusters is adjusted modestly to:

- `Initial Maximum Speed Increase = 25`;
- `Incremental Maximum Speed Increase = 20`.

For the 500f underlying max-power constant this corresponds to approximately:

- tier 1: 625;
- tier 2: 725;
- tier 3: 825;
- tier 4: 925.

This keeps the separate speed-cap progression relevant as the Jetpack reaches its cap faster, without doubling maximum power or altering JetpackFixes safety logic.
