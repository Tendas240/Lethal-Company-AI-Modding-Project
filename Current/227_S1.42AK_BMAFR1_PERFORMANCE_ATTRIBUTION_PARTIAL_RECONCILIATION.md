## Janitor correlation boundary

Janitor is not isolated as the object whose removal stopped the flood.

SpringMan spawns essentially concurrently with Janitor near flood onset. At the end, the last array error occurs after `Leaving current lobby` and host shutdown/disconnect but before both Janitor and SpringMan cleanup messages. Many objects are being torn down in the same window.

Accordingly, Janitor lifetime remains a temporal correlation and candidate mechanism only. The shared disconnect/cleanup window prevents a selective Janitor-death causal inference, and SpringMan remains a contemporaneous alternative candidate.
