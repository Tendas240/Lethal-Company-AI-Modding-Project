### Historical assumption — disproven

The plan assumed that identical positive, difficulty-independent EventType scale values would imply an exact normalized share of `1 / 8 = 12.5%` at the final EventType roll.

Fresh S1.42AC runtime evidence disproved that assumption. Five roll contexts consistently emitted final effective BCMER EventType weights:

`Insane 6843 / VeryBad 506 / Bad 355 / Neutral 1368 / Good 1244 / VeryGood 2737 / Rare 27375 / Remove 883`

Therefore equal configured scale curves are **not sufficient** to equalize final BCMER `1.71.0` EventType weights. BCMER applies additional type-dependent calculation/weighting.

The exact complete formula remains to be inspected; do not infer it solely from these runtime values.
