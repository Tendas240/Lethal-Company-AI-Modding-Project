EXACT BCMER 1.71.0 FINDING
======================================================================

Exact upstream source commit:
TheSoftDiamond/BrutalCompanyMinusExtraReborn@e2ca64b9954b7076e75e9a9ff97d76232c4086b0

At that commit Plugin.cs declares VERSION = "1.71.0".

UpdateAllEventWeights() computes:

p_i = computedScale_i / sum(computedScales)

perEventWeight_i = int(
    (eventTypeSum / fix(eventTypeCount[i]))
    * p_i
    * 1000
)

It then assigns perEventWeight_i to every individual MEvent of that type.

Meaning:
- `eventTypeCount[i]` = number of ENABLED events of the type;
- `eventTypeSum` = total ENABLED events;
- the logged `Set eventType weight for <type> to <value>` number is a PER-EVENT weight;
- it is NOT the aggregate probability mass of the EventType.

BCMER then selects individual MEvents by summing individual event weights.
Therefore the inverse `1 / eventTypeCount[i]` term intentionally compensates for unequal numbers of events per EventType.

For S1.42AC:
- every computed scale = 12.5;
- scale sum = 100;
- p_i = 0.125 for every EventType.

The unequal logged values are expected. They are consistent with BCMER distributing approximately equal aggregate EventType mass across types that have very different numbers of individual events.

The observed 219-event solution reconstructs:
- Insane 4 events;
- VeryBad 54;
- Bad 77;
- Neutral 20;
- Good 22;
- VeryGood 10;
- Rare 1;
- Remove 31.

After multiplying event count × per-event weight, aggregate static shares are about 12.4837% through 12.5070%; the tiny spread is integer truncation.
